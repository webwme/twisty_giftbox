Travelers who want a compact curl-care routine, people curious about Twisty who want to try before committing to full sizes, and anyone looking for a thoughtful haircare gift.

What curl types does it work for?

The products are formulated to work across wavy (type 2), curly (type 3), and coily (type 4) hair. Each product is designed to enhance natural texture without weighing hair down.

Can I take the box on a flight?

Yes. All liquid products are under 100 ml, making them carry-on friendly for most airlines. We recommend checking your specific airline's regulations before traveling.

How long will the minis last?

With regular use (2-3 times per week), the box provides approximately 4-6 weeks of product. Perfect for a month-long trip or for testing products before buying full sizes.

Absolutely. That's the whole point. Every product in the box is available in full size on our website, so once you find your favorites, you can easily reorder.
